The church modes are all here: Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Locrian, plus Ionian and Aeolian under their everyday names, major and natural minor. Dorian is the minor scale with a raised sixth — a favorite for jazz, funk, and modal rock. Compare A Dorian with A natural minor and you will see one dot shift: F becomes F sharp. That single color change is the Dorian sound.

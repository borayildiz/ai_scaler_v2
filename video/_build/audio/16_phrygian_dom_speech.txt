Phrygian dominant is the fifth mode of harmonic minor: a flat second and a major third. It is the sound of flamenco, metal, and a lot of Middle Eastern-influenced riffs. Hungarian minor raises the fourth of harmonic minor, which gives an extra pull toward the fifth. Double harmonic, also called Byzantine, has two augmented seconds and a very vocal, ancient quality. Select any of them and the red tonic still tells you where home is.

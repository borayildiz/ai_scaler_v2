Color is doing real work here. Blue dots are scale tones. Red dots are the root. That split sounds small, but on a crowded neck it is how you keep your place. In C major, every C on the board is red, and the rest of the major scale is blue. If you change the root to A, the red jumps to every A — the map rotates, the guitar does not. Look for red first, then walk the blues around it.

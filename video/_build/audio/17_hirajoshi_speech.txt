Japanese Hirajoshi and In Sen are five-note scales with a distinctly East Asian contour — great for melody that does not sound like a Western minor box. Prometheus is a six-note scale associated with Scriabin. Augmented, Neapolitan major and minor, and the Enigmatic scale round out the set. You do not have to know their history. Select one, and the neck shows you where to put your fingers. That is the point of a visualizer.

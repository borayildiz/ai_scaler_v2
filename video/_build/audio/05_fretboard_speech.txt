The neck is drawn the way most players picture it: the high E string at the top of the screen, the low E at the bottom. Open strings sit to the left of the nut, labeled with their pitch. Fret numbers run along the top. Inlay dots mark frets three, five, seven, nine, twelve, and fifteen — the same landmarks you feel on a real guitar. Only notes that belong to the current scale appear as dots. Empty wood means: do not land there, for this scale.

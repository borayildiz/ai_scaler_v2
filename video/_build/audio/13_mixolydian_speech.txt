Mixolydian is major with a flat seventh, which is the sound of dominant chords and a lot of rock riffs. Pick G Mixolydian and you are looking at the notes of a G seven chord, stretched across the neck. Lydian raises the fourth of major — bright and filmic. Phrygian flattens the second of minor — darker, Spanish, heavy. Locrian is the tense half-diminished mode, with a flat second and a flat fifth.

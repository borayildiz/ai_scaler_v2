Welcome to A.I. scaler — a desktop guitar companion that shows you any scale on a real fretboard, lists the notes, finds the related chords, and lets you hear everything. In the next ten minutes we will walk through the whole app: how to pick a root and a scale, how to read the board, how playback works, and how tunings and exotic scales fit in. You do not need to be a theory expert. If you can find the low E string, you can use this.

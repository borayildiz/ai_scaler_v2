Here is a simple way to use the app in a real practice session. Open a song you already play. Set the root to that key. Choose the scale the melody actually uses — minor pentatonic for a blues, Mixolydian for a dominant vamp, Dorian for a funk groove. Find every red root on the neck, then connect the nearest blue notes with short phrases. Click the related chords and strum them on the guitar, matching what you hear. Tomorrow, change one thing: a mode, an exotic color, or a different tuning. Small daily maps beat memorizing twenty box shapes you never use.
